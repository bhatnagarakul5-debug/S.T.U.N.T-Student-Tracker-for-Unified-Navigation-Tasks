DATA SOVEREIGNTY CHARTER

STUNT & J.A.R.V.I.S. Mark 58 Privacy Policy

Effective: September 30, 2026 • Lead Developer: Akul Bhatnagar

1. Zero Third-Party Telemetry: STUNT transmits zero analytics, tracking beacons, or behavioral telemetry to ad networks or data aggregators. Your academics remain completely confidential.

2. Local Storage by Default: Your course grades, attendance logs, timetables, syllabus notes, and financial ledger reside strictly on your local computer in stunt_database.db.

3. J.A.R.V.I.S. Mark 58 Isolation: J.A.R.V.I.S. communicates with STUNT through an isolated local IPC bridge on loopback address 127.0.0.1. Voice commands and file operations never execute on unapproved remote servers.

4. Anti-Shoulder Surfer Protocol: Pressing Ctrl+Shift+P in STUNT immediately masks sensitive attendance percentages, CGPA metrics, and financial records with '••••••' to protect your screen in public college libraries.

5. Instant Local Purge: You retain complete data sovereignty. Selecting 'Purge Local Database' or deleting the database file erases all records permanently with zero cloud remnants.
